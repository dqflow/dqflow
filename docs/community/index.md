# Community

dqflow is developed in the open on
[GitHub](https://github.com/dqflow/dqflow). Everything — the roadmap, the
adoption numbers, the launch plan — is public.

Discussions are the default for open-ended conversation. Issues are for a
specific, actionable bug or change.

## Maintainer response expectations

dqflow is maintained by a small team. What you can rely on:

- **Triage within about a week.** New issues get a label, a question, or a
  "yes, PR welcome" / "not planned, here's why".
- **No fix SLA.** A triaged bug is not a scheduled fix. Priority follows the
  [roadmap](https://github.com/dqflow/dqflow/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) and how many
  people it affects.
- **Pull requests reviewed within about two weeks.** Small, focused PRs with
  tests and a changelog entry move fastest. A draft PR early is welcome.
- **`good first issue` tickets are held** for first-time contributors for two
  weeks after someone says they are working on it.
- **Security reports are not filed in public.** Follow the process in
  `SECURITY.md` once it is published ([#66](https://github.com/dqflow/dqflow/issues/66)); until then, email the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`.

These are expectations, not guarantees — dqflow is pre-1.0 and volunteer-run.
